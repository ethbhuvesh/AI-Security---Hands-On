# Acme Corp Information Security Policy (Public Extract)

## Password and authentication
Employee passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180
days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access and for any
system that handles customer data.

## Data classification
Acme classifies data into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and
Restricted. Restricted data (for example, payment card numbers and government
identifiers) may never be pasted into third-party tools, including public AI
assistants, unless the tool is covered by a signed data-processing agreement.

## Acceptable use of AI tools
Employees may use approved AI assistants for drafting and research. They must not
submit Confidential or Restricted data to any assistant running on a free tier,
because free tiers may retain inputs for model training.

## Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one
hour of discovery. Do not attempt to remediate a suspected breach yourself
before the security team has been notified.
